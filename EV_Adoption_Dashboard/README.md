# Electric Vehicle Adoption Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze electric vehicle adoption trends in Connecticut.

The dashboard provides insights into vehicle registration patterns, geographic distribution, market leaders, consumer preferences, and adoption growth trends.

## Business Questions
- Which regions have the highest EV adoption?
- Which manufacturers dominate the EV market?
- What vehicle types are most popular?
- How has EV adoption changed over time?
- What demographic and geographic patterns exist?

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- Excel
- Data Visualization
- Business Analytics

## Dashboard Features

### Regional Distribution Map

Visualizes EV concentration across Connecticut and identifies high-adoption areas.

### Vehicle Popularity Analysis

Interactive ranking of:

- Manufacturers
- Models
- Vehicle Types
- Cities

### Adoption Trend Analysis

Tracks EV growth from 2008–2026 and highlights major adoption periods.

## Key Findings

- Tesla is the leading EV manufacturer.
- Model Y is the most popular EV model.
- Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) dominate registrations.
- Coastal and urban regions show higher EV adoption rates.
- EV adoption accelerated significantly after 2021.
